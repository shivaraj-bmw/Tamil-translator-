How it works

1. Your file is read entirely inside the browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

2. The moment text is extracted, translation starts automatically — no extra click.

3. If a PDF turns out to have no real text layer (common for scanned manga/manhwa chapters), it's automatically converted into pages and sent to the Manga translator instead.

4. Long files are split into sections and translated in parallel instead of one-by-one, so it finishes fast.

5. Only the language changes: structure, paragraph breaks, and order are kept exactly as in the original.

6. The result opens as a Tamil reading view — editable, downloadable as .txt, or printable to PDF.
